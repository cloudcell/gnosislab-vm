# GnosisLab Trademark Policy

`GnosisLab` is a trademark or potential trademark of Alexander Bikeyev.

Cloudcell Limited may use and administer the `GnosisLab` mark with the trademark
owner's permission.

This policy explains how the `GnosisLab` name and any official `GnosisLab` logos,
icons, slogans, and visual identity may be used by the community, contributors,
users, consultants, educators, and commercial partners.

This policy applies separately from the software license. The GnosisLab-VM
software may be available under the Apache License 2.0, but that license does not
grant trademark rights.

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

1. Protect users from confusion about the source, quality, and authenticity of
   GnosisLab-VM software and services.
2. Allow fair community use of the GnosisLab name.
3. Prevent misleading forks, rebranded distributions, or competing services from
   appearing to be official.
4. Preserve the reputation and identity of the GnosisLab project.

## Protected Mark

The protected marks are:

- `GnosisLab`
- `GnosisLab-VM`

This policy also applies to any official GnosisLab logo, icon, product badge,
splash screen, visual identity, or confusingly similar mark used in connection
with ML experimentation labs, sandboxed execution environments, VM templates,
or related software.

For clarity, this policy does not claim `ml-scientist`, `ml-anamnesis`,
`ml-episteme`, `ml-zetesis`, `ml-arete`, `ml-agora`, or other adjacent project
names as protected trademarks under this policy unless and until Alexander
Bikeyev separately publishes a policy or registration covering those marks.

## Permitted Uses

You may use the GnosisLab mark without prior written permission in the following
cases, provided that your use is truthful, non-misleading, and does not imply
endorsement by Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell Limited.

### Referring to the Project

You may refer to GnosisLab-VM by name when discussing, reviewing, teaching, or
describing the project.

Examples:

- "This tutorial explains how to use GnosisLab-VM."
- "Our research group runs its experiments in a GnosisLab lab."
- "This integration targets GnosisLab-VM."

### Community Discussion

You may use the GnosisLab name in blog posts, videos, documentation,
presentations, academic work, issue reports, and community discussion.

Examples:

- "GnosisLab-VM installation guide"
- "Notes on the GnosisLab-VM security model"
- "GnosisLab-VM template customization"

### Compatibility Statements

You may use the GnosisLab name to describe compatibility.

Examples:

- "Compatible with GnosisLab-VM"
- "Plugin for GnosisLab"
- "Template for GnosisLab-VM labs"

You must not imply that your plugin, template, integration, service, or product
is official unless Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell Limited has approved it in
writing.

### Forks

You may state that your project is a fork of GnosisLab-VM.

Example:

- "This project is a fork of GnosisLab-VM."

However, the fork must use a different name and branding unless Cloudcell
Limited gives written permission.

## Restricted Uses

You may not use the GnosisLab mark in a way that suggests your product, service,
fork, company, website, package, or distribution is official, endorsed,
sponsored, or maintained by Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell Limited unless you
have written permission.

The following uses require prior written permission from Alexander Bikeyev or
Cloudcell Limited.

### Rebranding or White-Labeling

You may not rebrand GnosisLab-VM, or any confusingly similar version of the
software, as your own official product.

Examples of prohibited use:

- Selling a modified version as "GnosisLab Pro" without permission.
- Releasing a fork called "GnosisLab Cloud" without permission.
- Releasing a fork called "GnosisLab Enterprise" without permission.
- Replacing the logo and presenting the software as your own official GnosisLab
  platform.

### Hosted or Managed Services

You may not use the GnosisLab mark for a hosted, managed, SaaS, or multi-tenant
service unless you have written permission from Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell
Limited.

Examples of prohibited use:

- "GnosisLab Cloud"
- "GnosisLab Hosted"
- "Managed GnosisLab Service"
- "GnosisLab-as-a-Service"

This restriction is separate from the software license. The Apache License 2.0 grants
copyright permissions to run, modify, and distribute the software, but it does
not grant permission to use Alexander Bikeyev's trademarks, product names,
logos, or brand identity.

### Competing Products

You may not use the GnosisLab mark, or confusingly similar marks, for a competing
commercial ML experimentation lab, sandbox platform, or VM-template product.

Examples of prohibited use:

- "GnosisLab Enterprise" for an independently operated competing product.
- "GnosisLab Labs" for a competing commercial platform.
- A fork branded so similarly that users may believe it is the official
  GnosisLab-VM project.

### Domain Names and Social Media Accounts

You may not register or use domain names, social media handles, app names,
package names, or organization names that imply official status.

Examples requiring permission:

- `gnosislab.cloud`
- `gnosislabpro.com`
- `gnosislab-platform.com`
- `github.com/gnosislab-enterprise`
- `@gnosislabcloud`

Community names are acceptable if they clearly avoid confusion.

Examples that may be acceptable:

- `gnosislab-notes`
- `awesome-gnosislab`
- `gnosislab-tutorials`
- `gnosislab-plugin-examples`

### Logos and Visual Identity

You may not use the GnosisLab logo, icon, splash screen, or visual identity as
the primary logo for your own project, product, company, fork, website, SaaS
platform, consulting service, or commercial offering.

You may use the official GnosisLab logo or icon in educational or descriptive
materials, provided that:

1. The use is accurate and non-misleading.
2. The logo or icon is not modified in a way that damages the brand.
3. The logo or icon is not used more prominently than your own name or
   branding.
4. The use does not imply endorsement or partnership.

## Naming Rules for Forks and Derivative Projects

If you publish a fork or derivative version of GnosisLab-VM, you must use a
distinct name.

Acceptable examples:

- "LabForge, a fork of GnosisLab-VM"
- "SandboxGrid, based on GnosisLab-VM"
- "Acme Experiment Lab, derived from GnosisLab-VM"

Unacceptable examples without permission:

- "GnosisLab Plus"
- "GnosisLab Enterprise"
- "GnosisLab Cloud"
- "GnosisLab Pro"
- "GnosisLab for Business"

Forks and derivative projects must also remove or clearly distinguish official
GnosisLab logos, splash screens, application icons, and product identity
unless otherwise permitted by Alexander Bikeyev or Cloudcell Limited.
